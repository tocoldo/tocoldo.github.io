Welcome to Tocoldo — your cozy corner of the internet dedicated to bite-sized gaming and the stories behind the code. Here you can explore a growing collection of mini-games, from classic logic puzzles like pong to fresh, original challenges you can jump into in seconds. But Tocoldo is more than just a place to play: it's also a peek behind the curtain. Our blog follows the development journey of each game — the ideas, the bugs, the design decisions, and the little victories that turn a simple concept into something fun. Whether you're here to kill a few minutes or to learn how games are made, you're in the right place.
